# The pitch round

Run it for unformed requests, and for "what should I talk about?" once topics are found.

## What to show

3 to 5 angles that are different from each other: a different claim, a different viewer, or a different emotion. Five rewordings of one idea is one angle. Fewer than three real angles: show fewer.

Each angle is exactly three lines:

```
1. <the angle, one sentence>
   Hook: "<the first spoken line>"
   Why now: <one sentence, grounded in a source> [n]
```

- The hook must be speakable on camera and stand alone, with no "today we're talking about".
- Why now names the event or date. If there is no real reason it is timely, say "evergreen" and don't invent one.
- Angle types worth mixing: a consequence ("this breaks X"), a how-to ("do Y in 2 minutes"), a contrarian take, a comparison, a prediction, a personal verdict.

## After showing

- Show all angles before recommending one. Then recommend one with a one-line reason.
- Mixing two is a valid answer: combine the hook of one with the point of another and re-state it.
- "You decide", "any", or "your pick" accepts the recommendation. Say which you took.
- Nothing fits: ask what was missing, once, and pitch again. Don't pitch a third round.

## Then

The chosen angle goes into `brief.md` as `angle`. Continue with the missing fields, the conflict check and the summary, as in SKILL.md. If the angles came from research, keep `research.md` and `sources.json` next to the brief so the script can cite them.
