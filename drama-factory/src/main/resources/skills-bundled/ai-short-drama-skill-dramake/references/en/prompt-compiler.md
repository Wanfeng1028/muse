# Video prompt compiler

A prompt is a generation contract compiled from approved story state, visual masters, coverage, and live model capability—not ad copy assembled from chat memory.

## Compiler inputs

- the block's `entry_state`, sole causal contribution, timed beats, and `exit_state`;
- long-term cast/location/prop/voice masters plus accepted short-term state;
- selected coverage, camera contract, and physical-event contract;
- freshly verified schema, limits, pricing, and mode incompatibilities;
- a reference-authority matrix with each asset's duty and forbidden transfer;
- MUST-HIT events, allowed deviations, P0 rejection conditions, and next seam.

Resolve conflicts or downgrade the route before compiling. Adjectives do not repair unknown state.

## Stable skeleton

Compress for the model's language and prompt limit while preserving this order:

1. `SCENE CONTEXT`: medium, place/time, exact cast count, current goal, and sole block contribution;
2. `ACTIVE REFERENCES`: bind identity, wardrobe, location, prop, voice, and motion duties plus `must_not_transfer`;
3. `LOCATION MAP & FIRST STATE`: starting geometry for subjects, camera, props, exits, and scale anchors;
4. `FORMAT & CAMERA`: aspect ratio, medium, height/lens, primary move, motivation, and endpoint;
5. `ACTION TIMING`: three to six timed beats with one primary change each; begin complex action already in progress instead of waiting in a pose;
6. `ACTING & PHYSICS`: speaker, listener response, contact phases, inertia, materials, and stable result;
7. `LIGHTING & AUDIO`: motivated sources, production sound, dialogue source, random-music boundary, and audio exit state;
8. `MUST-HIT & POSITIVE CONSTRAINTS`: one to three core events, exact exit state, and downstream handoff;
9. `NEGATIVE CONSTRAINTS`: only likely, high-loss failures—not dozens of competing negatives.

## Mode-specific compilation

- **T2V:** state identity and space more fully, but do not pretend text replaces risky character masters.
- **I2V/first frame:** do not renegotiate appearance and composition already owned by the frame; describe motion, acting, sound, and end state.
- **First/last frame:** define a feasible path from A to B without an unreachable location or costume change in between.
- **R2V/multi-reference:** bind every numbered input to one duty. Remove conflicts before adding references; count is not quality.
- **Extension:** continue only unfinished motion and future beats, using observed—not planned—parent exit state.
- **Editing:** separate preserved and changed layers; change one major layer at a time and specify time range, region/mask, and audio preservation.
- **Multi-shot/multi-prompt:** every sub-shot has entry, action, result, and sound; motivate cuts by action, eyeline, sound, or shape instead of packing unrelated locations together.

## Event load and downgrade

A 20–30 second limit does not make simultaneous rotating dialogue, handoffs, transformation, chase, location changes, and exact text reliable. Default to one primary prop system, one risky physics chain, and one payoff. If two candidates fail at the same timestamp:

1. keep MUST-HIT events and remove decoration;
2. simplify the camera or use a readable locked angle;
3. split setup, peak, and consequence;
4. use frozen keyframes, motion reference, or local editing;
5. only then change model or quality tier.

## Request preview and invalidation

Before paid execution, show model/surface, mode, duration, resolution, audio, full prompt, reference files and duties, expected cost, seed, candidates, and retry cap. Record version fingerprints for prompt, parameters, references, and schema.

After confirmation, any image- or cost-affecting change invalidates approval. Download path, filename, or a non-request note does not. Record the request ID immediately; after a timeout, query task history before resubmitting.
