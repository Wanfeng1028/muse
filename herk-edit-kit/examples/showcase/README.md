# Finished examples

## YouTube motion showreel

`youtube-showreel.mp4` is a 15-second showreel built with the `motion-showreel`
skill and supplied by Nate for publication: 1920x1080, 60fps, H.264 video and AAC
audio. Its generated lookdev shots came from Kie.ai, its music from Suno via
Kie.ai, and its sound effects from ElevenLabs. It is a design study, not an official
YouTube production.

## Short-form examples

These three videos were supplied by Nate for publication in this kit. All three are
1080x1920, with H.264 video and AAC audio. Press Play to watch here in the README.

The two "AI edits everything" reels were edited by an AI agent from Nate's one-take
recording, using the skills and the [editing playbook](../../docs/EDITING-PLAYBOOK.md)
in this kit. `ai-edits-everything-reveal.mp4` and
`ai-edits-everything-raw-vs-edited.mp4` are re-encoded from the 60fps finals to keep
the repository small (same resolution and frame rate, audio unchanged). The AIS Live
ad is an unchanged copy of the supplied export.
