# Intake

Send this in one message, in the person's language, adapted to what they already said. Group the questions; never trickle them one by one. Mark which are required.

## The message

**1. The video (required)**
- What is it about: a feature, a product, an announcement? What should someone remember after one view?
- Who watches it, and where will it be posted (LinkedIn, Instagram or TikTok, website, a stand screen, a deck)?
- What does it end on: a logo, a tagline, a call to action, or a seamless loop?
- Target length. Default: 15 to 25 seconds.

**2. The product on screen (required if the video shows a product)**
- Screenshots of every screen and state the video should show, or a link where they can be seen.
- The real labels, names and figures to display. Anything to anonymize (client names, people, numbers)?

**3. The design system (required, or say "use neutral defaults")**
- Colors as hex codes: background, text, accent.
- Fonts: the files (woff2, ttf, otf), or the Google Fonts names and I download them.
- The logo, as an SVG if possible.
- Style notes: corner radius, borders or shadows, a link to brand guidelines.

**4. An inspiration video (required, the most useful input)**
- A video whose feel you want: the file, or a link I can download it from.
- What you like in it: the pace, the transitions, the camera, the sound, the type. And what you don't.
- Optional: a written direction (rules, things to ban, a structure). It overrides what I read from the video.

**5. Formats and sound**
- Formats. Default: 1:1 (1440×1440) and 9:16 (1080×1920). 16:9 on request.
- Music: your own track (and whether you hold the licence to publish it), or a bed generated on the tempo with UI sounds placed on the beat (default).
- Will it autoplay muted? Then everything must read without sound.
- Real footage to include (people talking, product in use): the video files, and whether their sound should be heard. Do the people shown agree to appear?

**6. Copy**
- The language of on-screen text, and any exact sentence that must appear.

## Defaults when the person has no preference

| Item | Default |
| --- | --- |
| Palette | Warm off-white canvas `#F1EEE9`, ink `#121212`, white surfaces, one accent |
| Type | One clean sans (Inter or Geist), two weights |
| Length | 20 s at 120 BPM (10 bars), one-shot ending on the logo; the template's example is 12 s |
| Formats | 1:1 and 9:16 |
| Sound | Generated bed, UI sounds on cues |

## Turning answers into `brief.md`

Write one short section per item: subject and takeaway, audience and channel, the screens to recreate with their real labels, tokens and font files, the inspiration file with what to keep and avoid, formats, sound, copy. Add the style read (step 2), the approved grid (step 3) and, per delivered version, one line saying what changed. The next version starts from this file, not from memory.
