# Template catalog

All 30 templates render at 60 fps. Every piece of copy and every color is a variable, so
rebranding means filling in values, not editing code. Templates marked with photos ship
demo images made with Claude Imagine; swap them for your own product or people.

Pick by what the video has to do: a **Launch film** tells a short story that ends on the
product, a **Product demo** walks through screens, an **Ad** sells one idea fast, a
**Showreel** or **Logo reveal** shows off a brand, an **Explainer** walks through a
comparison or a process.
