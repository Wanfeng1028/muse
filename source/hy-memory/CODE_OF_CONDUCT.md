# Code of Conduct

The Hy Memory and MemHub community aims to provide a respectful, safe, and
inclusive environment.

Be specific, constructive, and considerate when discussing code, design, and
documentation. Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, and the
publication of another person's private data, credentials, or conversations
are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove issues, comments, or contributions that violate
these expectations and may restrict repeated offenders. Report sensitive
conduct or safety concerns through the private channel described in
[`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md); do not disclose personal information in a public
issue.
