Privacy

Last updated November 3, 2026

This policy explains what Native Cook knows about you, why, who else handles it, and what you can ask us to do with it. It applies to the site at nativecook.dev. The short version: we keep the little we need to run your account, we count visits without cookies or tracking, we run no ads, and we never sell data.

Native Cook is run by Ngoc Le, an individual based in Vietnam.

Email: support@nativecook.dev

1. What we collect

Account. Your email address, a display name, which sign-in method you use (email link, GitHub or Google) and when you signed in. If you sign in with GitHub or Google, they send us your email and name; we never see your password for those services. Email sign-in links are sent through Cloudflare on our behalf.

What you do on the site. The blocks you save. Your choices of theme and platform tab are kept in your browser only (see section 5).

Requests about a design. If you ask us to correct a credit or take a block down through the form on the credits page, we store what you send: your name, your email address, the block, the link to your original work and your message. You don’t need an account for that.

Technical records. The companies that host the site keep ordinary server logs (IP address, browser, pages and videos requested) for a short time for security and reliability, and that includes the requests to your account.

Visit statistics. To see how many people visit and which pages they use, we use Cloudflare Web Analytics. A small script on each page reports the page viewed, the site that linked to it, the type of browser and device, the country and how fast the page loaded. It sets no cookies, stores nothing in your browser and does not follow you across other sites, and we only see totals, never an individual visitor. We don’t use advertising or tracking tools.

2. Why we use it

  • To run your account, show you the right content and keep your saves: this is needed to provide the service you asked for.
  • To count visits and see which pages are used and how fast they load, from anonymous totals: our legitimate interest in improving the site.
  • To keep the site secure, prevent fraud and answer your emails: our legitimate interest in running the site properly.
  • To act on a request about a design and keep a record of what was changed or removed and why: our legitimate interest in crediting people correctly.

We only email you about your account. We don’t send marketing emails and we don’t sell or rent personal data.

3. Who else handles it

These companies process data for us, each only for the purpose listed:

  • Cloudflare (hosts the site and our database, so your account, sign-in and saves; stores and streams the preview videos, counts visits through Web Analytics, sends our sign-in emails, and receives email sent to our support address; data stored primarily in the Asia-Pacific region) — privacy policy
  • GitHub (sign-in provider if you choose it) — privacy statement
  • Google (sign-in provider if you choose it; our support mailbox is a Gmail account, so email you send us is kept there and our replies go out through it) — privacy policy

Some of them are outside your country, mainly in the Asia-Pacific region and the United States. They commit to safeguards such as standard contractual clauses for that data, and to processing it only on our instructions.

4. How long we keep it

Your account and saves stay until you delete your account, which you can do yourself on the account page or by emailing us from the address on the account. Server logs are kept for short periods and then discarded.

A request about a design is kept as the record of that decision. Ask us to delete yours and we will, once it has been dealt with.

5. Cookies and browser storage

The site sets one cookie of its own, only once you sign in: your sign-in session, which it needs to know it is you. It uses no tracking cookies (the visit statistics in section 1 use none either), so it shows no cookie banner. It keeps a few things in your browser’s local storage: your theme and platform choices, and the page to return to after signing in. Signing out ends the session; clearing site data in your browser removes all of them.

6. Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, to delete your account, or to object to how we use it. Email support@nativecook.dev from the address on your account; we answer as soon as we can and within the time the law gives us. If you live in the EU or the UK you can also complain to your data-protection authority. Residents of Vietnam have the rights set out in Decree 13/2023/ND-CP on personal data protection.

7. Children

Native Cook is for people aged 18 and over. We don’t knowingly keep data about anyone younger.

8. Security

Everything travels over HTTPS and our server only lets you read your own records. No method is perfect; if we learn of a breach that affects you, we will tell you.

9. Changes

When this policy changes we update the date at the top, and for meaningful changes we email account holders. Our terms live alongside this page.
