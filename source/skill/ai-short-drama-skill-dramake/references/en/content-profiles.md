# Content Profiles

Select one primary profile and combine only the specialist overlays that materially change production. The core story, continuity, evidence, cost, and audio gates remain the same.

## Live-action human drama

Use for family, romance, workplace, suspense, comedy, social-topic, and emotional shorts.

- Lock face, body, age, hairstyle, wardrobe topology, accessories, handedness, voice, and relationship knowledge.
- Stage dialogue as action and reaction; avoid standing in one room trading repeated exposition.
- Track eyelines, 180-degree axis, entrances/exits, phone orientation, prop hands, injuries, wetness, and emotional state.
- Use inserts, reactions, movement, and environment to convey information without malformed screens or documents.

## Pet or animal-led story

- The animal must causally affect the outcome.
- Use species-appropriate anatomy, senses, instincts, scale, contact, and locomotion.
- Lock one of `natural_photographic`, `heightened_realism`, or `animated_actor_hybrid`, then define permitted stance, paw gestures, expression range, action amplitude, and deformation bounds.
- In realistic/cute hybrids, keep the environment, fur, wardrobe, and props photographic. Animated-actor performance may exaggerate emotion and action but cannot break object count, contact, weight, or state continuity.
- Track coat maps, eye color, body proportions, collar/wardrobe construction, animal count, and human-animal bond before intervention.

## Anthropomorphic creature or fantasy

- Define the body plan and degree of anthropomorphism before scripting actions.
- Separate explicit magic rules from physical mistakes. Every power needs trigger, visible effect, limit/cost, and consequence.
- Keep scale, shadows, reflections, contact, and environment response consistent.

## Animation or motion comic

- Lock design grammar: line, shading, palette, proportions, frame/motion style, facial library, and background treatment.
- Do not apply live-action material negatives.
- Track pose-to-pose continuity, layer depth, camera grammar, speaking-frame rhythm, and reuse of canonical designs.

## Branded narrative or product story

- The product must solve or complicate a story problem rather than appear as a disconnected beauty shot.
- Lock pack shape, label-safe blank plates, color, material, scale, logo policy, claims, and legal constraints.
- Generate complex label/UI text as clean post overlays unless the selected image/video model is explicitly approved for it.

## UGC, talking-head, interview, or explainer

- Lock speaker identity, voice, lens/phone grammar, eye line, jump-cut style, room tone, and authenticity imperfections.
- Fill time with information change, gestures, examples, cutaways, or demonstrations—not empty camera movement.

## Multi-profile projects

Example: a live-action family drama with a magical animal uses `live-action human drama` as primary plus `pet` and `fantasy` overlays. Resolve conflicting rules explicitly in the production brief.
