# Review and editor handoff

Inspect the opening, fully typed request, send frame and following frame, thread opening, longest result fields, approval target, save receipt, colleague reply, final reactions, and final frame. Seek directly to each state to check determinism. Inspect the actual clean and wrapped composition if delivering both.

Look for text crossing rails, clipping in fields, oversized synthetic avatars, scroll content escaping its panel, a cursor that misses a resized control, premature receipts, and insufficient read time. Check the longest copy at a reduced viewing size. A compiler pass cannot establish legibility.

Render the complete clip and check decoded dimensions, fps, duration, and full decode. Direct vector rendering at scale 2 produces a 3840×2160 output; a raster avatar remains limited by its original source. A screenshot or capture placed in the composition retains its native detail and acquisition cadence. Label transcodes and upscaled copies accurately.

Suggested handoff:

- `01_Final_Video`: accepted viewing MP4 and requested master.
- `02_Demo_Assets`: clean raw UI, approved original recordings, and posters.
- `05_Code`: active source, lockfile, config, registry, and exact render command.
- `06_Editor_Notes`: event table, representation disclosure, asset provenance, and media report.

State that conversations, agent progress, approvals, reactions, and saved results are illustrative wherever that is true. A clean clip may omit an in-frame disclosure for editing; retain the disclosure alongside it. For footage that really came from Slack, document the workspace authorization and the recorded product version without bundling credentials or private messages.
